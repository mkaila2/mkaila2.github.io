Product Management · Enterprise Software

Making transportation software work better.

Supporting product improvements for Edge TMS at Werner Enterprises, where small workflow details can make a big difference for enterprise users.

OrganizationWerner Enterprises
RoleProduct Management Intern
FocusRequirements · Stakeholders · Workflows
The product mindset
Good enterprise products should reflect how people actually work.

Context

During my product management internship at Werner, I worked on the Edge transportation management system (TMS), including its Commitments functionality. The work required understanding operational workflows and coordinating across product and technical perspectives.

The challenge

Transportation workflows have many real-world variations. Product requirements and validation rules need to accommodate legitimate use cases while helping teams maintain reliable data and predictable behavior.

What I contributed

  • Worked with product stakeholders to understand requirements and workflows around the Commitments feature.
  • Supported investigation and communication of functionality or workflow gaps.
  • Translated observations into actionable follow-up for product and technical partners.

What I learned

Product management is often about making complex workflows legible—asking targeted questions, clarifying edge cases, and helping different stakeholders align on what the product should do.
