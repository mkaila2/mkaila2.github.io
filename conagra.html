Rethinking a 10,000-record review with AI.

Exploring how AI-assisted analysis could reduce manual effort in an urgent enterprise data-quality workflow.

OrganizationConagra Brands
RoleData Management Intern
FocusMRO records · Copilot · Data quality
Core question
When the dataset is too large for a quick manual pass, how can we review it more intelligently?

Context

While supporting Conagra's data management organization, I encountered an urgent MRO material-review task involving approximately 10,000 rows of records and descriptions. Potential duplicates needed to be identified efficiently.

The challenge

Manually reviewing every row under time pressure would have been labor-intensive. I looked for a way to make the first pass more efficient while keeping the review focused on potential duplicate records.

My approach

  • Structured the material-review problem around identifying likely duplicate descriptions and records.
  • Created a Copilot-based AI agent using relevant knowledge sources to help flag potential duplicates.
  • Combined AI-assisted analysis with a review process rather than treating model suggestions as confirmed matches.

Product thinking

The key decision was to focus the workflow on identifying candidates for review—not assuming an AI model could make every final data-quality judgment. That distinction matters for enterprise reliability.

What I learned

AI is most useful when it fits a clearly defined workflow, reduces repetitive work, and leaves room for human validation where accuracy matters.
